💡 About the lights

Static, Dynamic and Imported choose which objects a bulb lights, so they do nothing to room geometry. They are here to match Tomb Editor. Shadow is a point light with a negative intensity, so set Intensity below zero.

Drag a coloured handle to move the selected light. A Sun lights by direction, so moving one only changes where its shadows fall: aim it with the yellow handle instead.

A Spot uses In α and Out α for its cone, and shades the surface by the way it points rather than by the direction back to the bulb. That is measured against Tomb Editor, not assumed.

Time of day swings the first Sun along an arc and re-lights as it goes. The angle is measured; Warm at the horizon is ours, not Tomb Editor's, so untick it to see the sun you actually built at the angle the slider picked.

A flame is the only light that reaches room geometry after the bake, so it is the only one that moves without re-lighting. Carrying one around the room is the quickest way to see what the material maps are doing.

A flame lights through walls, and so does the game: TombEngine casts no shadows from a room's dynamic lights.
